Rachneet Kaur

(new draft intro)

I am a Vice President and AI Research Lead at J.P. Morgan AI Research in New York, where I lead research on multimodal AI agents. My work aims to build agents that (i) see and reason over visually rich content such as charts, slide decks and financial reports, and (ii) adapt quickly to new tasks, tools and data modalities, including time series. Some recent work:

  • ChartAgent and SlideAgent ACL 2026: agents for visually grounded chart reasoning and multi-page document understanding.
  • AdaptAgent ACL 2025: adapting multimodal web agents from a few human demonstrations; LETS-C ACL 2025: language embeddings for time series classification.
  • 10 filed patents and a JPMorgan Chase Prolific Inventor award (2025); organized 4 workshops and a tutorial at AAAI and ACM ICAIF.

I received my Ph.D. in Industrial Engineering from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, advised by Richard Sowers and working closely with Manuel Hernandez, Justin Sirignano and Roy Campbell. There I built multimodal machine learning for digital health, from vision-based gait analysis for multiple sclerosis and Parkinson's disease to wearable sensors and virtual reality. This work was recognized with the William A. Chittenden II Fellowship (three years) and the Sharp Outstanding Graduate Student Award.

Before my Ph.D., I earned an M.Sc. in Mathematics from IIT Delhi and a B.Sc. (Honours) in Mathematics from the University of Delhi. During my masters, my thesis was advised by Aparna Mehra. Along the way I interned at J.P. Morgan AI Research, Visa Research, 3M and Quantlab, and did research internships at Université Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne and TIFR.

About Me

I am currently a Ph.D. student in the Department of Industrial Engineering at the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign. I am advised by Richard Sowers and also collaborated closely with Manuel Hernandez. My current research is focused on applications of machine learning for unfolding problems in health and finance domain.

Prior to joining PhD, I have earned a B.S. (with honours) in Mathematics from the University of Delhi and an M.S. in Mathematics from the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT), Delhi. During my masters, my thesis was advised by Aparna Mehra.
